Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Use light-to-moderate weights, controlled reps and good form. Do not add exercises. Day 7 is rest.

Nutrition · 7 days

Avoid: added sugar/sweets, sugary drinks, junk food, maida products and alcohol.

Choose home-cooked foods: eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, dal, curd, milk; roti, rice, oats, poha, upma, sweet potato; vegetables; ghee, nuts, seeds, coconut; fresh fruit (limit 2/day).

Water: minimum 3 litres/day. The Phase 1 PDF does not prescribe calories, macros, portion sizes or meal timings.

Supplements · optional

The PDF lists whey 1 scoop (25–30 g) post-workout, creatine monohydrate 5 g post-workout, multivitamin with breakfast, fish oil 1000 mg with breakfast, and D3 + K2 (5000 IU D3) with a meal. Supplements are optional in Phase 1; check suitability and dosage with a clinician/pharmacist, especially for vitamin D and existing medicines.

Avoid spending on fat burners, testosterone boosters, unnecessary pre-workouts, BCAAs if protein intake is adequate, and mass gainers.

Warm-up and cool-down

Use the warm-up/cool-down routine document as a movement reference. It gives push, pull and leg warm-ups and stretches; Phase 1's workout plan itself is the same full-body routine each of Days 1–6. Warm up before training; cool down after training.
